# Chai at 3:07, Ep 1: The Stall That Wasn't There

*A tea stall appears on a mountain highway at 3:07 every night. Everyone who stops there is about to die on the road, and tonight it's Kabir's turn.*

**Format:** series episode · **Platform:** YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels · **Aspect:** 9:16 · **Runtime:** 0:47 (7 shots)

## Why this video

- Trend: Local horror tied to a real kind of place: haunted highway stories (811K) and festival ghost stories (4.5M) lead this week's scan
- Trend: Series labelling and cliffhanger titles outperform the scan median
- Trend: Micro-dramas of about a minute are found through feeds; few horror channels use that format
- Our twist: The haunted stall is the only safe place on the road. Its ghost is trying to save the living, and each episode Kabir must keep one doomed stranger from driving away before 3:08.

## Series: Chai at 3:07, episode 1

**Season arc:** Kabir learns why the stall chose him, and who Noor was before the road took her.

**Cliffhanger:** Kabir's own truck drives past the stall with Kabir at the wheel, heading for the broken bridge.

## Story

**Opening hook (first 3 seconds):** A dashboard clock flips to 3:07 and a lit tea stall appears in the fog where there was only cliff a second ago.

**Other openings to test:**
- Open on Kabir's own truck roaring past with Kabir at the wheel, freeze, then text: 'One minute earlier.'
- Open on Noor sliding the glass across the counter: 'Your tea, Kabir.' before we know who either of them is.

**Hook:** The stall appears out of nothing at 3:07.

**Warning:** Noor already has Kabir's tea poured and tells him not to leave before 3:08.

**Proof:** He laughs it off, walks back toward his truck, and sees it drive past with himself inside.

**Ending:** Kabir stands frozen at the stall holding the glass as his own truck's tail lights vanish toward the broken bridge. Noor says only: 'Now you believe me.'
