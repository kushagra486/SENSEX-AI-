## Series: Result Day, episode 1

## Story

**Opening hook (first 3 seconds):** At 4 a.m. a woman's hands bury a thick textbook inside the family's rice container.

**Other openings to test:**
- Open on the admit card held over the gas flame, freeze, then text: 'Baarah ghante pehle.'
- Open on Sarla telling the neighbour 'ek roti gol nahi banti' while, behind her, Meera silently mouths an answer from memory.

**Hook:** The book goes into the rice.

**Secret:** Meera studies before dawn and hides it when the house wakes.

**House:** A normal day: taunts from Sarla, a husband who does not look up.

**Stakes:** The exam is six days away and the admit card is folded in her saree.

**Discovery:** Sarla finds the books and the admit card.

**Ending:** Sarla holds the admit card above the flame and asks how this happened behind her back; Meera stands frozen in the doorway.
